Current Job Openings: Greater Toronto Area

Essential Job Functions

  • Machinery Installation: Expertly install, align, and assemble machinery and equipment, ensuring all components are securely in place and function correctly.
  • Maintenance and Repairs: Conduct routine maintenance and repair tasks on machinery and mechanical systems to keep them running efficiently and safely.
  • System Troubleshooting: Diagnose mechanical problems and implement solutions promptly to minimize downtime.
  • Component Fabrication: Utilize tools and machinery to fabricate and modify parts as necessary for equipment upkeep and improvements.
  • Preventive Maintenance: Develop and implement preventive maintenance schedules to prolong the life of machinery and avoid unexpected breakdowns.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with other team members to ensure machinery operations align with company standards and production goals.
  • Compliance and Safety: Adhere to all safety protocols and regulations to maintain a safe working environment.

Experience Required

  • Certification: Must hold a valid Millwright certification.
  • Professional Experience: At least three years of experience in an industrial setting performing installation, maintenance, and repair of machinery.
  • Technical Skills: Proficient in using hand and power tools, machinery, and equipment for maintenance tasks.
  • Problem-Solving Abilities: Strong troubleshooting skills with the ability to diagnose and resolve mechanical issues effectively.
  • Communication Skills: Excellent verbal and written communication skills to interact with team members and document maintenance activities.
  • Mechanical Aptitude: Demonstrates mechanical inclination with a good understanding of machinery operation and maintenance.
  • Safety Knowledge: In-depth knowledge of safety regulations and best practices in an industrial environment.
  • Driving Record: Must maintain a good driving record for any necessary travel or on-site work.
