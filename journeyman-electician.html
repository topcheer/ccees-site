Journeyman Electrician

Current Job Openings: Greater Toronto Area

Essential Job Functions

  • Electrical System Installation and Maintenance: Set up and sustain various electrical systems, including EMT, PVC, and rigid conduit, cable tray, instrumentation and controls wiring, new service installations, and lighting setups.
  • Blueprint Interpretation: Analyze and comprehend electrical schematics or blueprints to ensure accurate execution of projects.
  • Control Panel Assembly: Construct and wire electrical control panels meticulously to meet project specifications.
  • System Troubleshooting and Repairs: Diagnose and rectify electrical system failures, including machinery breakdowns and power issues, to restore functionality swiftly.
  • Code Compliance: Guarantee that all electrical installations adhere to local electrical code standards.
  • Independent Work Management: Exhibit self-organization and the capability to perform tasks efficiently with minimal supervision.

Education and Experience Required

  • Electrical Certification: Must possess a valid 309A electrical license with a background in industrial or commercial projects.
  • Professional Experience: Minimum of three years working as a licensed 309A electrician is essential.
  • Motor Control Expertise: Familiarity with motor control systems is advantageous.
  • Communication and Service Skills: Exceptional ability in communication and customer service is crucial.
  • Code Knowledge: Comprehensive understanding of the Ontario and Canadian Electrical Codes is necessary.
  • Driving Record: Good Driving Record
